# Idea Inbox

[Index](INDEX.md) · [Capabilities](CAPABILITIES.md)

Capture unapproved possibilities without presenting them as commitments. Record
only the problem/opportunity, desired outcome, key constraints/questions and
eventual disposition.

After explicit spec approval, link the change from the capability's approved
target. Remove duplicated detail here. Rejected or merged ideas may retain one
short disposition when useful; durable rationale belongs in decisions.
